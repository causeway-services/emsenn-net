Brave Old World Podcast

The Brave Old World Podcast as a podcast where emsenn was the podcast host and dungeon master in a Dungeons & Dragons campaign

Original Copy

Brave Old World is a make-believe world world that my friends hang out in while I serve as the narrator. We use numbers and dice rolls to help make decisions but mostly it’s up to our collective imagination. Put another way, we’re a Dungeons & Dragons podcast.

Whenever we play, we stream the session live on Twitch. Those get edited and get released as a podcast.

Brave Old World is like one of those old-time radio plays, only we use chance to decide where we’re going, instead of a script. Six of my friends are cast as the heroes, and I’m playing the extras.

The story so far

The Brave Old World used to be a land of magic and wonder. Humanity was entering a golden age after discovering a Eastland populated with elves and halflings. Their wars with the dwarves and orcs of the Old World ended.

swords-shield-bow80 years ago, magic vanished in what would be called the Collapse. The dwarves disappeared and the elves became feral. Humanity was alone and cascaded into war.

wrench-scales-briefcaseWar and wealth created the spark of an industrial revolution. Industrialization brought bureaucracy and a new peace, held by debt and credit. Humanity successfully coped with the Collapse.

That’s where our story begins. Meet the cast…
