# Evaluate Free-Work Offer

- Ask the collaborator to specify the exact criteria for free work.
- Ask: why are they not currently doing this work for someone who already meets those criteria?
- Clarify scope, boundaries, and expected outcomes before accepting.
- Record the agreement in writing.
