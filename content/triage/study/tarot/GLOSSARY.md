# Tarot Glossary

## major arcana

The 22 trump cards representing archetypal themes.

## minor arcana

The 56 suit cards divided into four suits and ranks.

## spread

A layout that assigns interpretive roles to card positions.

## orientation

Whether a card appears upright or reversed.
