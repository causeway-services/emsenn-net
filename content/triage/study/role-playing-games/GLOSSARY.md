# Role-Playing Games Glossary

- **character**: The fictional persona a player portrays in the game.
- **campaign**: A connected series of sessions.
- **consent**: Agreement among players about what content is acceptable in play.
- **game master**: A facilitator who describes the world and resolves outcomes.
- **safety tool**: A method for signaling boundaries or discomfort during play.
- **session**: A single play meeting.
- **setting**: The fictional world where the game takes place.
- **system**: The rules and mechanics used to resolve actions.
