# Scenes and Choices

Audience: new players learning how play flows.

Learning goal: describe the scene-choice-consequence loop.

A session proceeds in scenes where characters face a situation, make choices,
and face consequences. Rules help resolve uncertain outcomes, but the focus
remains on shared narration.

Check for understanding: What happens after a character makes a choice in a
scene?
