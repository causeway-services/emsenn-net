# Domestic Microgreens and Sprouts Glossary

- **cotyledon**: The first leaves that appear when a seed sprouts; many microgreens are harvested at this stage.
- **food-safe**: Suitable for contact with food without introducing harmful chemicals or residues.
- **rinse cycle**: A routine of rinsing seeds or sprouts with clean water to reduce microbial growth.
- **sanitize**: To reduce microbes on tools, jars, or trays using approved cleaning steps.
- **seed hull**: The outer shell of a seed, which often detaches during sprouting.
- **sprout**: A germinated seed harvested before true leaves develop.
- **tray**: A shallow container used to grow microgreens in a thin layer of medium.
