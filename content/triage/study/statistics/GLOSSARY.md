# Statistics Glossary

- **bias**: A systematic error that shifts estimates away from the truth.
- **distribution**: The pattern of values a variable takes.
- **mean**: The arithmetic average of values.
- **median**: The middle value when data are ordered.
- **mode**: The most frequent value in a dataset.
- **population**: The full set of items or people under study.
- **sample**: A subset of the population used for measurement.
- **standard deviation**: A measure of how spread out values are.
- **variable**: A measurable property or feature.
- **variance**: The average squared deviation from the mean.
