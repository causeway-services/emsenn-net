# Tabletop Role-Playing Games Glossary

- **dice mechanic**: A method for resolving uncertainty using dice or tokens.
- **initiative**: A rule for sequencing actions during conflict.
- **modifier**: A bonus or penalty applied to a roll.
- **session zero**: An initial meeting to align expectations and create characters.
- **table culture**: Shared norms for how players interact.
