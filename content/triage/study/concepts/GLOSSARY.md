# Concepts Glossary

## concept module

A module organized around a concept with theory, practice, and tools.

## genealogy

A record of how a concept emerged and was shaped.

## conceptual genealogy

A Foucauldian method for tracing how concepts are made possible by discourse,
institutions, and power relations, focusing on contingencies and discontinuities
instead of origins.

## history

A record of how a concept has changed or been used over time.

## primary intension

The descriptive content that fixes a concept's reference in a context.

## secondary intension

The actual referent of a concept given how the world is and how the concept was fixed.
