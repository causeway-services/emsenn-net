# Logic Glossary

## Proposition

A statement that is either true or false.

## Formula

A well-formed expression built from symbols according to grammar rules.

## Connective

A logical operator such as "and", "or", or "implies".

## Quantifier

Symbols that express "for all" ($\forall$) or "there exists" ($\exists$).

## Model

A structure that assigns meaning to symbols so that formulas have truth values.
