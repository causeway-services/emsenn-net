# Stratified Directed Infinity-Sites Glossary

## Stratification

A decomposition of a space or category into layers (strata) ordered by refinement or dimension.

## Directed

Having morphisms with a preferred direction; not all morphisms are invertible.

## Infinity-site

An $\infty$-category equipped with a Grothendieck topology, enabling higher sheaf conditions.

## Working definition

In this repository, a stratified directed $\infty$-site is an $\infty$-site whose objects are layered by a stratification and whose morphisms respect a directed structure.

## Stratum

One layer in a stratification.

## Covering family

A family of morphisms that covers an object.

## Refinement

A map to a more detailed or smaller stratum.
