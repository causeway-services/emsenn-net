# Homotopy Theory Glossary

## Homotopy

A continuous deformation from one map to another.

## Homotopy equivalence

A map that has an inverse up to homotopy.

## Fundamental group

The group of loops based at a point, up to homotopy.

## Homotopy group

A generalization of the fundamental group to higher-dimensional spheres.

## Path

A continuous map from an interval into a space.

## Homotopy type

The equivalence class under homotopy.

## Contractible

Homotopy equivalent to a point.
