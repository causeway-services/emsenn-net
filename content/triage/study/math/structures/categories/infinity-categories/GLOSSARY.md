# Infinity Categories Glossary

## infinity category

A category-like structure with morphisms in all dimensions, not just objects and
arrows.

## simplicial set

A combinatorial object made of simplices and face maps, used to encode higher
categorical data.

## horn

A simplicial subset missing one face, used to express higher composition laws.

## quasi-category

A simplicial set where every inner horn has a filler; a common model of infinity
categories.

## equivalence

A higher-categorical notion of isomorphism that is invertible up to higher
homotopies.
