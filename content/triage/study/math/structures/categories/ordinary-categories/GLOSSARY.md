# Ordinary Categories Glossary

## Object

A thing in a category: a set, type, state, or context.

## Morphism

A directed arrow or process from one object to another.

## Composition

The operation of doing one morphism after another.

## Identity morphism

The "do nothing" morphism for an object.

## Functor

A structure-preserving map between categories.

## Natural transformation

A systematic way to transform one functor into another, compatible with all morphisms.

## Category

Objects and morphisms with identity and composition.

## Isomorphism

A morphism with an inverse.

## Commutative diagram

A diagram where all paths with same endpoints agree.
