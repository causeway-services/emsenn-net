# Topology Glossary

## Topological space

A set $X$ together with a collection of open sets that satisfies the topology axioms.

## Open set

A subset that is declared "open" and participates in unions and finite intersections.

## Basis

A collection of open sets such that every open set is a union of basis elements.

## Continuous map

A function that pulls back open sets to open sets.

## Closed set

The complement of an open set.

## Homeomorphism

A continuous bijection with continuous inverse.

## Compactness

Every open cover has a finite subcover.
