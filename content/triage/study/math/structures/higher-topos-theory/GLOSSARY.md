# Higher Topos Theory Glossary

## Infinity-category

A category where morphisms have morphisms between them, continuing to all higher levels, with associativity up to coherent homotopy.

## (Infinity,1)-category

An infinity-category where all $k$-morphisms for $k>1$ are invertible up to higher morphisms.

## (Infinity,1)-topos

A presentable $(\infty,1)$-category that behaves like a category of sheaves of spaces.

## Sheaf of spaces

A sheaf valued in spaces (homotopy types) rather than sets.

## Geometric morphism

A pair of adjoint functors between topoi that preserves the relevant structure.

## Site

A category equipped with a Grothendieck topology.

## Sheaf condition

Local data that glues uniquely to global data.

## Adjunction

A pair of functors related by universal properties.
