# Sheaf Theory Glossary

## Site

A category with a Grothendieck topology.

## Cover

A family of morphisms into an object that are declared to be its local pieces.

## Presheaf

An assignment of data to objects with restriction maps along morphisms.

## Sheaf

A presheaf where compatible local data can be uniquely glued into global data.

## Restriction

The operation of moving data from a larger context to a smaller one.

## Glue

To combine compatible local data into one global piece of data.
