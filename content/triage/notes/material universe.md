The material universe is the collective term for all [[enc/def/matter\|physical matter]], [[enc/def/energy\|energy]], and [[space\|space]] that is [[relating\|relating]] together.

It includes celestial bodies like [[star\|stars]], [[planet\|planets]], [[galaxy\|galaxies]]; [[phenomenon\|phenomena]] such as [[gravity\|gravity]], [[light\|light]], and [[time\|time]]; and the [[fundamental particles\|fundamental particles]] that make up [[atom\|atoms]]. This vast expanse relates through [[physics\|physics]] and encompasses everything from the largest galactic structures to the smallest subatomic particles. The material universe is where [[life\|life]] takes form, [[civilization collapse\|civilizations collapse]], and where you probably are as you're [[reading\|reading]] this.
