# Outline Semiotic Universe Data

- Specify the semantic domain `H` and list its Heyting operations.
- Describe the modality `j` and trace `G`, including required laws.
- List the primitive syntactic operators and their arities.
- State the interpretation conditions (monotone, join-continuous, modal, trace, fragment-preserving).
- Identify how fragments are generated and how fusion is defined.
