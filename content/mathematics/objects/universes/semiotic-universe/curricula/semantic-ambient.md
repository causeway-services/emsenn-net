The semiotic universe begins with a semantic domain `H` that supports logical
structure and temporal structure at once.

## Complete Heyting algebra

`H` is a complete Heyting algebra. This provides:

- a partial order `<=`,
- meets and joins for any subset,
- an implication operator that encodes logical entailment.

## Modality and trace

Two additional operators act on `H`:

- `j` is a modal closure operator (monotone, extensive, idempotent, join-preserving).
- `G` is a trace comonad that also preserves Heyting operations.

Together they describe a semantic system with modal stability (`j`) and temporal
unfolding (`G`).
