# Derive Interaction Terms From Markdown

- Treat headings as interaction term constructors with their subsection content.
- Convert links into terms that reference other thing handles by target path.
- Map inline math and definitions into semantic generator terms.
- Keep the interaction surface finite by selecting the most salient terms.
