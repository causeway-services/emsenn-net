Interactive semioverse models assume both semantic structure and semiotic
interpretation.

## Semantics

Semantic theories explain how references are fixed and stabilized.

## Semiotics

Semiotic theories explain how signs generate interpretants in context.

## Application

Things, interaction terms, and footprints can be interpreted as signs and
interpretants operating over a structured semantic domain.
