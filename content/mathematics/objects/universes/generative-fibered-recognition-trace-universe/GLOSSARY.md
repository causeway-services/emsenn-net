# GFRTU Glossary

## Trace site
A small category with a Grothendieck topology that indexes traces and their refinements.

## Recognition fiber
A finite Heyting algebra attached to each trace, equipped with stabilizer and drift maps.

## Stabilizer
An idempotent, monotone endomorphism on a fiber representing stable recognitions.

## Drift
A monotone endomorphism that moves recognitions along a temporal or dynamical axis.

## Fixed fiber
The subset of a fiber fixed by both stabilizer and drift.

## RTL
Recognition Term Language, the syntax interpreted in the sheaf topos.

## Sheaf universe
The sheaf topos on the trace site, where recognitions assemble as sheaves.

## Closure operator UG
The generative closure operator on objects of the sheaf universe.

## Cell
A local universe produced by restricting the trace site to a subcategory.

## Trace

A finite record of an interaction or process.

## Grothendieck topology

A specification of covering families.

## Fiber

Local recognition structure at a trace.
