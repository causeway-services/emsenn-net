A quasi-category is a simplicial set in which every inner horn has a filler.
This condition ensures that higher composition exists up to coherent homotopy.

## Intuition

- Vertices represent objects.
- Edges represent 1-morphisms.
- Higher simplices encode higher morphisms and their coherence data.

Quasi-categories are a widely used model for infinity categories because they
translate higher categorical structure into combinatorial data.
