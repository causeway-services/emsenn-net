Ordinary categories capture objects and morphisms, but many constructions in
homotopy theory require higher morphisms that witness equivalences.

Infinity categories add morphisms in every dimension so that composition and
equivalence are tracked up to higher homotopies, not strict equality.

This gives a setting where the objects of interest are equivalent in a flexible
way while still supporting categorical reasoning.
