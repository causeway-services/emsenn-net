Key concepts in pedagogy, with definitions grounded in the critical and decolonial traditions this module draws from.

- [banking model](banking-model.md) — Freire's characterization of conventional education as depositing knowledge into passive learners
- [conscientization](conscientization.md) — the development of critical awareness and the capacity to act on it
- [dialogic education](dialogic-education.md) — learning through dialogue between co-investigators rather than transmission from authority
- [land-based education](land-based-education.md) — education rooted in sustained relationship with place
