## Assumed audience

- Reading level: general adult.
- Background: basic biology helpful.
- Goal: understand the organism you will grow.

## What they are

Oyster mushrooms are a group of edible fungi in the Pleurotus family.
They grow on wood and break it down.

## Why they are popular

- Fast growing.
- Tolerant of different substrates.
- Good flavor and texture.

## Life cycle in one paragraph

Spores start mycelium. Mycelium spreads through a food source. When
conditions are right, it forms clusters of mushrooms (fruiting bodies).
