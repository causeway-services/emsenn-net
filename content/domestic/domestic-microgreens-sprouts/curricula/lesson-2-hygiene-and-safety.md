Audience: beginners who want to avoid food safety problems.

Learning goal: list the key hygiene steps for safe microgreens and sprouts.

## Clean tools and surfaces

Wash trays, jars, and lids with hot soapy water, then rinse well. Let them dry
before the next batch.

## Rinse and drain

For sprouts, rinse seeds and growing sprouts with clean water 2-3 times per day.
Drain fully so water does not pool.

## Watch for spoilage

Discard batches with off odors, slimy texture, or unusual discoloration. When in
doubt, throw it out.

Check for understanding: Why is draining as important as rinsing for sprouts?
