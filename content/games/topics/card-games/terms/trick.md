---
title: "trick"
aliases:
  - "trick"
date-created: 2026-03-01T00:00:00
---

A unit of play, common in trick-taking games, where each player contributes one
card and a winner is resolved by rule. Trick structure controls pacing,
information reveal, and scoring flow. Many advanced strategies are timing
strategies about when to concede a trick versus when to force one.
