Audience: players new to tabletop role-playing.

Learning goal: describe how tabletop RPGs differ from digital RPGs.

Tabletop RPGs are played face-to-face or online with shared rules, dice or
tokens, and a facilitator. The group negotiates outcomes together rather than
relying on a computer to enforce rules.

Check for understanding: What role do dice or tokens play in tabletop RPGs?
