Audience: newcomers to tabletop and digital role-playing.

Learning goal: define role-playing games and name the core roles in play.

Role-playing games are structured play where participants portray characters and
make choices within a shared fiction. A facilitator (game master) frames scenes
and resolves uncertainty using a rules system.

Check for understanding: What is the role of the game master in a session?
