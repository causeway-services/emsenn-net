Audience: groups establishing shared boundaries.

Learning goal: explain why safety tools and consent matter in play.

Table culture includes agreements about tone, content, and boundaries. Safety
tools help participants communicate discomfort or hard limits before and during
play, keeping the table respectful.

Check for understanding: Why are safety tools useful even among friends?
