Audience: new players preparing their first character.

Learning goal: list the core elements of a character sheet.

Most role-playing games define a character with a concept, goals, and mechanics.
Common elements include attributes, skills, and equipment. Players should align
character goals with the shared tone of the game.

Check for understanding: What is one way to align a character with the tone of
an RPG campaign?
