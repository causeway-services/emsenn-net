---
title: "suit"
aliases:
  - "suit"
date-created: 2026-03-01T00:00:00
---

A categorical grouping of cards within a deck, such as hearts, diamonds, clubs,
and spades in common French-suited decks. Suits often govern legality (for
example follow-suit rules), comparison order, and signaling. In games without
suits, an equivalent category system may still perform the same structural role.
