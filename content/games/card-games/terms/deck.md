---
title: "deck"
aliases:
  - "deck"
date-created: 2025-12-31
---

A bounded set of cards used as the core material of play. A deck can be
standardized (such as a 52-card French-suited deck) or custom to a specific
game. Deck composition determines probability landscape, action density, and
strategic depth. Changing deck size or card distribution is a major rules
change, not cosmetic flavor.
