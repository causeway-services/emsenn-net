Audience: groups preparing to run their first session.

Learning goal: outline the typical phases of a tabletop RPG session.

A session often includes a recap, scene framing, play with conflict resolution,
and a closing debrief. Many groups begin with a session zero to establish safety
and expectations.

Check for understanding: Why is a session zero useful for a new group?
