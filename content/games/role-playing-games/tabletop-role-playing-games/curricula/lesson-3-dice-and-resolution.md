Audience: players learning how outcomes are decided.

Learning goal: explain how dice mechanics resolve uncertainty.

Dice mechanics map actions to outcomes by comparing a roll to a target number or
opposed roll. Modifiers represent character skill or situation.

Check for understanding: What does a modifier represent in a dice roll?
