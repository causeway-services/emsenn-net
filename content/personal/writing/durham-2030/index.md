***Durham 2030*** is a (type:: [[speculative fiction setting\|speculative fiction setting]]) representing an [[imaginging\|imaginging]] of [[Durham\|Durham]] in the year [[2030\|2030]]. Written in [[Winter 2023\|Winter 2023]], the fiction assumes that [[TESCREAL\|TESCREAL]] ideology has reshaped local settlements into [[technofascist\|technofascist]] enclaves known as [[arcologies\|arcologies]].

This page serves as an overview of the fiction.
