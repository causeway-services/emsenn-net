## Interpretation

Complementing meet, we need the least recognition containing a pair.  *Order Recognition* witnessing *Contextualize Trace* produces ***Join Recognition*** ([JoinRecognition](join-recognition.md)).

*Contextualize Trace* already records how recognitions extend; composing with the order yields the corresponding join, so *Join Recognition* follows.
