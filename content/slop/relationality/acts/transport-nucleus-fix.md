## Interpretation

A similar transport holds for nucleus-closed recognitions.  *Morph* witnessing *Fix Nucleus* produces ***Transport Nucleus Fix*** ([TransportNucleusFix](transport-nucleus-fix.md)).

This composition records ν'(f(x)) = f(ν(x)), so nucleus-fixed recognitions map to nucleus-fixed ones.
