# Trace Stratum

Trace acts assemble the oriented tree mechanics: entry/exit roles, head/tail
operations, and the renewal loops that make recorded traces behave coherently.
They translate the foundational witnesses into reusable traversal structure.

Acts classified under **trace** grouped by phase.
