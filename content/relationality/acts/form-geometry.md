# Shaping Geometry

## Interpretation

A geometry is the semantic structure equipped with compatible nuclei and flow.  *Structure Semantics* witnessing *Commute *Flow* And Nucleus* yields ***Form Geometry*** ([FormGeometry](form-geometry.md)).

This composition records that the designated nuclei and flow satisfy the compatibility laws on the semantic calculus, so *Form Geometry* follows.
