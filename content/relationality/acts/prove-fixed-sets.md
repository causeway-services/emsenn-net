# Proving Fixed Sets Lemma

## Interpretation

*Flow*-fixed recognitions remain closed under the meet operations already earned.  *Fix Flow* witnessing *Meet Recognition* yields ***Prove Fixed Sets*** ([ProveFixedSets](prove-fixed-sets.md)).

This composition records that invariants stay closed under the *Meet* operation, so *Prove Fixed Sets* is valid.
