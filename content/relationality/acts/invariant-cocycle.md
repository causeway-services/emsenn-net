# Invariant Cocycle

A cocycle is a cochain whose coboundary collapses under the fixed nucleus.
We capture this by letting the coboundary witness the nucleus:
`(InvariantCoboundary FixedNucleateProfile)` earns
***InvariantCocycle***.
