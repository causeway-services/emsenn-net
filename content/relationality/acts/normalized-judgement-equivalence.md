# Equating Normalized Judgement

## Interpretation

Finally, *Normalized Judgement* must remain indistinguishable to all contexts.  *Equate Contexts* witnessing *Normalized Judgement* yields ***Normalized Judgement Equivalence*** ([NormalizedJudgementEquivalence](normalized-judgement-equivalence.md)).

This act enforces the indistinguishability, so *Normalized Judgement Equivalence* is valid.
