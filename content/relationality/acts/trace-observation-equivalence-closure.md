# Closing Trace ObservationEquivalence

## Interpretation

After reentry, the entire trace must close under this equivalence.  *Trace Reentry Closure* witnessing *Reenter Observation Equivalence* produces ***Trace Observation Equivalence Closure*** ([TraceObservationEquivalenceClosure](trace-observation-equivalence-closure.md)).

This act closes the trace with the reentered equivalence, so *Trace Observation Equivalence Closure* is established.
