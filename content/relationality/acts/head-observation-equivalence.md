# Heading ObservationEquivalence

## Interpretation

The sequence also needs its stabilized head.  *Head Trace* witnessing *Tail Observation Equivalence* produces ***Head Observation Equivalence*** ([HeadObservationEquivalence](head-observation-equivalence.md)).

Using earned recognitions, this isolates the immediate equivalence, so *Head Observation Equivalence* stands.
