# Composite Profile Nucleus

## Interpretation

Nuclei likewise restrict to the composite profile.  *Nucleate* witnessing *Profile Composite* produces ***Nucleate Composite Profile*** ([NucleateCompositeProfile](nucleate-composite-profile.md)).

The act records the inherited closure on the composite domain, so *Nucleate Composite Profile* is valid.
