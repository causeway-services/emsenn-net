Audience: beginners learning about Reiki practice.

Learning goal: outline common practice steps and boundaries.

Reiki practice often includes grounding, focused attention, and gentle hand
placements. Practitioners set boundaries by emphasizing consent and clarifying
that Reiki is a complementary practice rather than medical treatment.

Check for understanding: Why is consent emphasized in Reiki practice?
