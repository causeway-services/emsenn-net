## Assumed audience

- Reading level: general adult.
- Background: no math required.
- Goal: understand information as a measure of uncertainty.

## The core idea

Information is what reduces uncertainty. If you already know the outcome, you
learn nothing new. If you are surprised, you gained information.

## A simple example

- If a coin is fair, a flip gives you one bit of information.
- If a coin always lands heads, a flip gives you zero bits of information.

## Why this matters

Information theory is a way to measure how much we learn from messages, data,
or observations.
