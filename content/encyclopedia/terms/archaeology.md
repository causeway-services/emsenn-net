# Archaeology (Method)

Archaeology is a method for analyzing the historical layers of discourse without
assuming a linear progress of ideas. It examines how statements, concepts, and
rules of formation define what is sayable in a given period.

## Key features

- focuses on the rules that govern discourse in a period
- analyzes statements rather than author intentions
- emphasizes discontinuity between historical epochs
