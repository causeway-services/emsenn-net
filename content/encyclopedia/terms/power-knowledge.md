Power/knowledge is the idea that power and knowledge are mutually reinforcing.
Power shapes what can be known, and knowledge stabilizes power by making certain
views and practices authoritative.

## Implications

- expertise can function as a form of social control
- institutions stabilize knowledge through rules and training
- critique often targets the link between authority and truth claims
