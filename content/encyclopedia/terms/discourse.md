# Discourse

Discourse refers to the systems of language, practice, and institutional rules
that shape what counts as knowledge in a field. Discourses organize what can be
said, who can say it, and what is treated as true or false.

## Key features

- links language to institutional practice
- defines boundaries of acceptable knowledge
- changes over time through shifts in power and expertise
