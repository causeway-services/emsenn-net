# Foucault Glossary

- **archaeology**: A method for analyzing the rules that govern discourse in a period.
- **biopower**: Power that manages life at the level of populations.
- **discourse**: Systems of language and practice that define what counts as knowledge.
- **genealogy**: A method for tracing concepts through contingencies and power relations.
- **governmentality**: Techniques and rationalities for governing populations.
- **power/knowledge**: The idea that power and knowledge reinforce one another.
