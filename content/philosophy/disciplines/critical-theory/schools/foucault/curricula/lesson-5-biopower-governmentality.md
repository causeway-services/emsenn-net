# Biopower and Governmentality

Audience: readers learning Foucault's political concepts.

Learning goal: describe how institutions manage populations and conduct.

Biopower describes power over life through norms, statistics, and public health.
Governmentality describes the rationalities and techniques used to shape
conduct, often through expertise and policy.

Check for understanding: What is one way governmentality shapes behavior?
