Governmentality refers to the rationalities and techniques by which populations
are governed. It includes how authorities shape conduct through policy, norms,
and expert knowledge, not only through direct law or force.

## Key features

- emphasizes governing through institutions and expertise
- links political power to knowledge about populations
- includes everyday techniques of administration
