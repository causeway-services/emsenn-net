Genealogy is a critical method for tracing how concepts and practices emerge
through historical contingencies rather than from a single origin. In a
Foucauldian sense, it analyzes institutions, discourses, and power relations that
make a concept possible at a given time.

## Key features

- focuses on discontinuities, breaks, and shifts in meaning
- treats concepts as historically situated rather than timeless
- links ideas to institutions, expertise, and authority
- emphasizes how power shapes what can be known or said

## Related figures

- Michel Foucault
- Friedrich Nietzsche (as a genealogical influence)
