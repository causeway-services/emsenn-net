# Genealogy

Audience: readers learning Foucault's historical method.

Learning goal: describe genealogy as tracing contingencies and power relations.

Genealogy explains how concepts emerge from historical contingencies rather than
from a single origin. It links ideas to institutions, power, and practices that
stabilize meaning.

Check for understanding: Why does genealogy avoid searching for a pure origin?
