# Archaeology

Audience: readers learning Foucault's methods.

Learning goal: explain archaeology as an analysis of discourse rules.

Archaeology studies how statements become possible in a period by examining the
rules that govern what counts as a valid statement. It does not focus on author
intentions, but on the conditions that make statements appear.

Check for understanding: What does archaeology study instead of author intent?
