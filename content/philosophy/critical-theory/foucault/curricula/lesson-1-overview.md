# Foucault Overview

Audience: readers new to critical theory.

Learning goal: describe Foucault's focus on knowledge, power, and institutions.

Foucault analyzed how institutions and discourses shape what counts as truth. He
is known for methods called archaeology and genealogy, and for concepts like
power/knowledge, biopower, and governmentality.

Check for understanding: What is one question Foucault asks about knowledge?
