- Only use allusions if the target genre explicitly permits them.
- Prefer no allusions unless the piece is in a reflective or essayistic genre.
- Approved sources for allusions:
  - The Golden Girls
  - MASH
  - Don Quixote
  - The Bible
  - The Quran
  - The Torah
- Use at most one allusion per 800 words unless the genre definition says otherwise.
- When used, make the allusion intelligible without external knowledge.
