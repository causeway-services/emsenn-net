- Identify the genre and its feeling rules (what emotions are expected or constrained).
- State the intended affect in one sentence before drafting.
- Avoid mixed affect signals unless the genre explicitly supports them.
- Use concrete sensory language only when it serves the intended affect.
- Use [genre-allusions](/media/writing/tools/genre-allusions/SKILL.md) if allusions are permitted.
- When unsure, prefer restraint over intensity.
