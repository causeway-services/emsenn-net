# Semantics and Semiotics Bridge

The semiotic universe formalizes a semantic domain but depends on semiotic
notions of signs and interpretants to justify how syntax is interpreted.

## Semantics input

- Intension and extension motivate how semantic elements are fixed.
- Primary and secondary intension explain how references stabilize in context.

## Semiotics input

- Signs and interpretants justify treating syntax as a sign system.
- Semiotic relations motivate fragmentwise coherence and fusion.

## Bridge in the semioverse

The semantic domain provides structure; the semiotic layer explains why the
structure is interpreted and closed in the ways the semioverse requires.
