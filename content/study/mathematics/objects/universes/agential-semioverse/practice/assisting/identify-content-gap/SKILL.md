- Choose a target area (e.g., study domain, agent, or specification).
- List the required artifacts (curriculum, glossary, skills, examples).
- Compare the required artifacts to what exists.
- Record each missing artifact as a gap with a short rationale.
- Convert each gap into an idea using
  [write-idea](/mathematics/objects/agential-semioverse/practice/planning/write-idea/SKILL.md).
