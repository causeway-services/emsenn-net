# Semiotic Universe Glossary

## complete Heyting algebra

A complete lattice with implication where meets and joins exist for all subsets.

## modality

A closure operator `j` that is monotone, extensive, idempotent, and preserves
joins.

## trace comonad

A comonad `G` on the Heyting algebra that also preserves the Heyting operations.

## fragment

A finitely generated modal-temporal Heyting subalgebra of the semantic domain.

## fragment-preserving

An operator that maps each fragment back into itself.

## fusion

A closure operation that identifies syntactic operators when their semantic
interpretations agree on all fragments.

## semiotic structure

The pair of semantic data and syntactic operators equipped with the closure
operators that generate the semiotic universe.
