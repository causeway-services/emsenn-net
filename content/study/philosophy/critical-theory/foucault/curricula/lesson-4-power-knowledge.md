# Power/Knowledge

Audience: readers learning Foucault's core claims.

Learning goal: explain how power and knowledge reinforce each other.

Power and knowledge are co-constitutive: institutions produce knowledge that
stabilizes authority, and that authority shapes what counts as knowledge.

Check for understanding: How can expertise function as a form of power?
