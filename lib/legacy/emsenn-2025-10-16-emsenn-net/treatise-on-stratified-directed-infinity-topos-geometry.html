Treatise on Stratified Directed Infinity-Topos Geometry

Treatise on Stratified Directed ∞-Topos Geometry

Preliminaries and Model Framework

Ambient Model

We model ∞-categories by quasi-categories, i.e. simplicial sets with the Joyal model structure.

External (co)limits, Kan extensions, and fibrations are computed in this setting.

Internal reasoning occurs within a fixed cohesive ∞-topos \(\mathfrak{E}\).

Let \((\Sigma \dashv \square \dashv \Pi)\) be the standard cohesive adjoint triple of modalities satisfying:

  1. \(\Sigma\) and \(\Pi\) are left- and right-exact;
  2. \(\square : \mathfrak{E} \to \mathfrak{E}\) is a left-exact reflective modality with unit \(\eta : \mathrm{id} \to \square\);
  3. Each modality is definable in the internal language of \(\mathfrak{E}\).

All internal ∞-categorical constructions below are interpreted in \(\mathfrak{E}\) via its homotopy type theory (HoTT).

Logical Conventions

  • \(\|{-}\|\) denotes propositional truncation.
  • \(\simeq\) denotes homotopy equivalence (internal to \(\mathfrak{E}\)).
  • “Locally constant” and “coherent” are defined with respect to the internal logic of \(\mathfrak{E}\).

Reference Frameworks

  1. Lurie – Higher Topos Theory (HTT)
  2. Schreiber – Cohesive ∞-Topoi

Components

Stratified Directed Bases

Axiom, Stratified Directed ∞-Site (A1)

A stratified directed ∞-site is a tuple \((\mathcal{X}, S, p, \square)\) where:

  1. \(\mathcal{X}\) is an ∞-category with Grothendieck topology \(\mathcal{J}\);
  2. \(S\) is a finite poset (of strata);
  3. \(p:\mathcal{X}\to S\) is a stratification functor, whose fiber \(\mathcal{X}_\sigma:=p^{-1}(\sigma)\) is a full sub-∞-category;
  4. \(\square:\mathcal{X}\to\mathcal{X}\) is a left-exact reflective endofunctor with unit \(\eta:\mathrm{id}\Rightarrow\square\) satisfying:
    1. (Directedness) \(\mathrm{Im}(\square)\) is a wide subcategory stable under pullback and composition, with all non-identity morphisms non-invertible;
    2. (Strata Monotonicity) if \(\sigma\le\tau\) in \(S\), then \(\eta\) restricts \(\mathcal{X}_\sigma\to\square(\mathcal{X}_\tau)\) and is $p$-monotone.

A morphism \((\mathcal{X},S,p,\square)\to(\mathcal{Y},T,q,\square')\) is a continuous functor \(f:\mathcal{X}\to\mathcal{Y}\) preserving covers, strata (via a monotone map \(S\to T\)), directed maps, and equipped with a modal natural isomorphism \(f\circ\square\simeq\square'\circ f\).

Lemma, Exit ∞-Functor

Let \((\mathcal{X},S,p,\square)\) be a stratified directed ∞-site. Define the exit ∞-category \(\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})\): \[ \mathrm{Map}_{\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})}(\sigma,\tau) := \underset{x\in\mathcal{X}_\sigma,\,y\in\mathcal{X}_\tau}{\mathrm{colim}} \mathrm{Map}_{\mathcal{X}}(x,\square y). \] Then \(\mathrm{Exit}_\infty\) extends to a functor \[ \mathrm{Exit}_\infty:\mathbf{StratDir}_\infty\to\mathbf{Cat}_\infty. \]

Lemma, Skeletal Filtration

If \(S\) carries a rank function \(\dim:S\to\mathbb{N}\), then \(\mathcal{X}\) admits a filtration \[ \mathrm{Sk}_0\mathcal{X}\subset \mathrm{Sk}_1\mathcal{X}\subset\cdots\subset\mathcal{X}, \] where \(\mathrm{Sk}_n\mathcal{X}:=p^{-1}(\{\sigma\mid\dim(\sigma)\le n\})\).

Part III. Constructible and Coherent Diagrams

Definition, Constructible Diagram

Given \((\mathcal{X},S,p,\square)\) and a stable presentable $∞$-category \(\mathcal{E}\) internal to \(\mathfrak{E}\), define \[ \mathrm{Constr}(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{E}) := \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{loc}}_{\square} (\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X}),\mathcal{E}), \] the ∞-category of locally constant and $\square$-covariant diagrams.

Definition, Coherent Diagram

A diagram \(F\in\mathrm{Constr}(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{E})\) is coherent if for every object \(x\) in \(\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})\) the canonical map \[ \eta_x : F(x) \to \mathrm{hocolim}\,F \] is an equivalence in \(\mathcal{E}\).

3.3 Assumption (Exit Cofinality)

\(\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})\) is weakly contractible, for instance if \(S\) has a maximum stratum and every \(\sigma\) admits a directed morphism \(\sigma\to\tau_{\max}\).

3.4 Theorem (Coherence Criterion)

If \(\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})\) is weakly contractible and \(F\) is locally constant, then for any \(x\), \[ F(x)\simeq\mathrm{hocolim}\,F. \]

Part IV. The ∞-Topos of Coherence

4.1 Definition (∞-Topos of Coherence)

Let \(\mathfrak{E}_{\mathcal{X}}\) be the full sub-∞-category of \(\mathrm{Constr}(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{E})\) consisting of coherent diagrams. It is an ∞-topos, realized as the left-exact accessible localization \[ \mathfrak{E}_{\mathcal{X}} \;\simeq\; L_S\mathrm{Constr}(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{E}), \] where \(L_S\) inverts the set of canonical maps \(\{\eta_x\}\).

4.2 Proposition (Stability Under Directed Morphisms)

For any directed natural transformation \(\Phi:F\to F'\) in \(\mathfrak{E}_{\mathcal{X}}\), \[ \eta_x\ \text{equivalence for }F \;\Rightarrow\; \eta_x\ \text{equivalence for }F', \] and \(\mathrm{hocolim}\,F\to\mathrm{hocolim}\,F'\) is a modal equivalence.

4.3 Definition (Field Object)

Assuming \(\mathcal{E}\) complete, define the field object \[ \mathcal{F}:=\mathrm{holim}_{F\in\mathfrak{E}_{\mathcal{X}}}F. \]

4.4 Axiom A2 (Ambidextrous Invariance)

If \(\mathcal{E}\) is symmetric monoidal stable with dualizable objects of \(F\), then \[ \mathrm{holim}\,\mathcal{F}\simeq\mathrm{hocolim}\,\mathcal{F}, \] expressing modal fixed-point equivalence for \(\square\).

4.5 Corollaries
  1. (Global–Local Equivalence) For coherent \(D\), \(\mathrm{holim}\,D\simeq\mathrm{hocolim}\,D\).
  2. (Stability Under Kan Extension) Coherence is preserved by directed Kan extensions.

Part V. Duality and Ambidexterity

5.1 Definition (Dual Diagram)

Let \(\mathcal{E}\) be symmetric monoidal stable. For \(F:\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})\to\mathcal{E}\) with pointwise dualizable values, define \[ F^{\vee}:\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathcal{E}, \quad F^{\vee}(x):=\underline{\mathrm{Hom}}(F(x),1). \]

5.2 Theorem (Verdier Ambidexterity)

If \(\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})\) is finite and \(F\) pointwise dualizable, then \[ \mathrm{hocolim}\,F\simeq\mathrm{holim}\,F^{\vee}. \] Proof. By coend–end duality in a symmetric monoidal stable category and the adjunction \[ \mathrm{Map}(1,\mathrm{holim}\,F^{\vee}) \simeq \mathrm{Map}(\mathrm{hocolim}\,F,1). \]

5.3 Corollaries
  1. \(F\) coherent ⇔ \(F^{\vee}\) coherent;
  2. Dualization induces a contravariant equivalence

\[ (-)^{\vee}:\mathfrak{E}_{\mathcal{X}}^{\mathrm{op}}\simeq\mathfrak{E}_{\mathcal{X}}. \]

Part VI. Dynamics (Kan Extensions and Fibrations)

6.1 Definition (Homotopy Kan Extensions)

Given \(\Phi:\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})\to\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{Y})\) and \(F:\mathrm{Exit}_\infty(\mathcal{X})\to\mathcal{E}\), define \(\mathrm{LKan}_\Phi F\) and \(\mathrm{RKan}_\Phi F\) by the standard universal properties.

6.2 Lemma (Stability Under Cofinal Maps)

If \(\Phi\) is cofinal (resp. coinitial), then \[ \mathrm{hocolim}\,F\simeq\mathrm{hocolim}\,\mathrm{LKan}_\Phi F \quad (\text{resp. }\mathrm{holim}\,F\simeq\mathrm{holim}\,\mathrm{RKan}_\Phi F). \] Hence coherence is preserved under such \(\Phi\).

6.3 Definition (Condition Fibration)

Define the condition fibration \[ \mathrm{Cond}:\mathfrak{E}_{(-)}\to\mathbf{StratDir}_\infty \] as the Grothendieck construction of the pseudofunctor \(\mathcal{X}\mapsto\mathfrak{E}_{\mathcal{X}}\) with reindexing \(f_!:={\mathrm{LKan}}_f\).

6.4 Theorem (Dynamic Invariance of \(\mathcal{F}\))

Under A2 and 6.2, \(\mathrm{holim}\,\mathcal{F}\simeq\mathrm{hocolim}\,\mathcal{F}\) is stable up to modal equivalence for all directed \(\Phi\).

Part VII. Axiomatic Summary and Meta-Framework

7.1 Axioms

  • A1 (Stratified Directed Cohesive Base)
  • A2 (Modal Ambidextrous Invariance)
  • A3 (Verdier Duality under Dualizable Targets)
  • A4 (Cohesive Ambidexterity)

7.2 Derived Theorems

T₁ (Coherence Criterion) T₂ (Verdier Ambidexterity) T₃ (Dynamic Invariance)

7.3 Model Interpretations

  1. Cohesive Topoi: stratified directed manifolds with conical stratifications.
  2. Modal HoTT: types with directed modal operator \(\square\).
  3. Directed Simplicial Sets: \(\mathrm{Exit}_\infty\) interpreted as Grandis’ $d$-spaces.
