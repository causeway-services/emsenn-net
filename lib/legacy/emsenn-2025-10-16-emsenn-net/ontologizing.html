ontologizing

Ontologizing is conceptualizing the mode of existence of a thing.

In relational dynamics, ontologizing is conceptualizing a possible secondary intension of a thing, expressing that intension as a Fregean first-order predicate, testing it as a Tarskian model-theoretic truth, and reflexively revisiting earlier stages of the ontologizing process as necessary to achieve ontological closure.
