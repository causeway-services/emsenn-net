ontology

An ontology is a theory of being.

It is the structure that is the result of ontologizing.

This structure structures the categorizing of being.

In relational dynamics, an ontology is one or more claim of relation, which may be understood as secondary intension expressed as a Fregean first-order predicate to establish a Tarskian model-theoretic truth.
