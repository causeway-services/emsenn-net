Red Union calendar

The Red Union calendar is the calendar used by the Red Union.

The Teraumian year has 365.25 days, which is the same as the year on Earth.

In this calendar are split into 12 30-day Teraumian month with 5 holidays coming between certain months, and an extra holiday every four years (Teraumian leap year)

Month / Holiday Equivalent Description
Deepwinter January Most places are frozen, except Gnalens, which is mid-50s
Midwinter holiday Christmas Religious holiday, second gift-giving holiday
Teraumian Winter February Most places are frozen, except Gnalens and Halfling Country
Sunsets March Teraumian spring equinox is on the 19th of Sunsets. Ack thaws
Storms April Central Plains thaw
Greengrass Easter Planting festival, gorging on the last reserves of winter
Melting May Farsteppes thaw
Flowers June Teraumian summer solstice is on the 20th of Flowers
Summertide July Farfarsteppes thaw
Midsummer   Every four year, a day called Shieldmeet happens after Midsummer.
Highsun August Farfarsteppes harvest, Constructive Criticism Day is on the 15th of Highsun.
Fading September Teraumian autumn equinox is on the 21st of Fading. Farfarsteppes freeze
Highharvesttide Thanksgiving This is the gift-giving, big feast, big holiday. Everyone celebrates.
Leaffall October Farsteppes freezes.
Rotting November Central Plains freeze
Feast of the Moon   Various regional celebrations
Drawing December Teraumian winter solstice is on the 20th of Drawing
