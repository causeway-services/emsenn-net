Proto-Germanic language

#+date [2025-08-12 Tue 05:49]

The Proto-Germanic language was a language spoken between approximately 500bce and 200ce in southern Scandinavia and northern Germany.

Proto-Germanic language developed from Proto-Indo-European language, differentiated by several features, described by the following:

  • Grimm's Law indicated how consonants shifted.
  • Verner's Law indicated exceptions to Grimm's Law.
