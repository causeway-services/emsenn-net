---
description: Apply the authorial voice of emsenn to a piece of writing.
date-created: 2025-04-01
---

- Use first-person singular ("I") when the author defines, motivates, or explains.
- Use inclusive first-person plural ("we", "let's") for shared derivations or worked examples.
- Avoid ambiguous "we"; name the subject if it is not author+reader.
- Write respectfully: do not tell the reader how to feel, and avoid reducing people to qualities.
