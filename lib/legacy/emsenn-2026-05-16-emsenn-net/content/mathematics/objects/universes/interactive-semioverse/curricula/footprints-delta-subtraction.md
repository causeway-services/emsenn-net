With interaction surfaces in place, we can define how a thing affects the
semantic field and how to compare or remove that influence.

## Footprint

The footprint of a thing is the closure of its thing fragment using the state's
operators. It yields a closed partial state associated with the thing.

## Delta

Delta measures difference between interaction surfaces, fragments, or
footprints. It captures how much two things diverge in their semantic effects.

## Subtraction

Subtraction removes semantic influence. It is defined using Heyting subtraction
and fragment generation so that removal stays inside the closure discipline.
