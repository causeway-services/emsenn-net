Biopower is a form of power that manages life at the level of populations.
Rather than focusing only on punishment or law, it operates through norms,
statistics, public health, and administrative practices.

## Key features

- targets populations rather than individual bodies
- relies on measurement, surveillance, and regulation
- connects political authority to life and health
