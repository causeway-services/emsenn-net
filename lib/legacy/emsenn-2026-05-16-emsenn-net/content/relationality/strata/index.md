Overview of every stratum (act counts are current as of the original specification).

1. [[foundation|Foundation]] — 15 acts. Seeds the primordial recognitions extracted from Relationlessness.
2. [[trace|Trace]] — 33 acts. Establishes oriented trace mechanics (entry, head/tail, renewal).
3. [[term|Term]] — 32 acts. Builds syntax, contexts, and fixpoint constructors from the trace layer.
4. [[observation|Observation]] — 36 acts. Records how values and traces are witnessed, normalized, and compared.
5. [[judgement|Judgement]] — 16 acts. Formalizes judgement triads and their interaction with observations.
6. [[semantics|Semantics]] — 125 acts. Introduces the algebraic order/meet/join and morphisms interpreting judgements.
7. [[stability|Stability]] — 9 acts. Captures the invariants needed to freeze traces, terms, and contexts.
8. [[geometry|Geometry]] — 34 acts. Couples flows with nuclei to describe relational geometry and residuation laws.
9. [[context|Context]] — 4 acts. Evolves, collapses, and packages contexts under the flow-nucleus stack.
10. [[dynamics|Dynamics]] — 7 acts. Studies phase spaces, mixing, and ergodicity of the flow-measure evolution.
11. [[profile|Profile]] — 34 acts. Develops filters, disciplines, and nested profile towers.
12. [[physics|Physics]] — 47 acts. Connects profiles with observables, states, and physical evolution.
