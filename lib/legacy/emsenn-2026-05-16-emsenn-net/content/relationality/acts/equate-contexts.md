# Equating Contexts

## Interpretation

Stabilization alone does not tell whether contexts can distinguish two normalized terms.  *Recognize Context* therefore witnesses *Normalization*, yielding ***Equate Contexts*** ([EquateContexts](equate-contexts.md)).

Composing these recognitions records indistinguishability across contexts, establishing *Equate Contexts*.
