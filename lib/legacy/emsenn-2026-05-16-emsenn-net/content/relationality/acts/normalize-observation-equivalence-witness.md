# Witnessing Normalized ObservationEquivalence

## Interpretation

Evaluated equivalence observations must normalize back to the *Witness Observation Equivalence*.  Hence *Normalization* witnesses *Evaluate Observation Equivalence*, producing ***Normalize Observation Equivalence Witness*** ([NormalizeObservationEquivalenceWitness](normalize-observation-equivalence-witness.md)).

This composition records the normalization of the equivalence evaluations, so *Normalize Observation Equivalence Witness* is valid.
