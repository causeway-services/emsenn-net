# Systematizing Physics

## Interpretation

A physical system couples the profile’s dynamics with its observables.  *Physics Profile* witnessing *Observe* produces ***Systematize Physics*** ([SystematizePhysics](systematize-physics.md)).

This composition binds the observable family to the inherited dynamics, so *Systematize Physics* follows.
