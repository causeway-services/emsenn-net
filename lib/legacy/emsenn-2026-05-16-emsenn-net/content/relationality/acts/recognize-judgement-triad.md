# Recognizing Judgement Triad

## Interpretation

The trace exists, yet we lack the recognition that presents a traced triad as a single judgement.  *Type Application* therefore witnesses *Trace Judgement Triad*, producing ***Recognize Judgement Triad*** ([RecognizeJudgementTriad](recognize-judgement-triad.md)).

This use of earned recognitions records the triadic judgement, so *Recognize Judgement Triad* is valid.
