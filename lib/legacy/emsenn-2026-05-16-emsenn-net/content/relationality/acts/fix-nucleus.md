# Fixing Nucleus

## Interpretation

The nucleus determines which recognitions are closed, but we must identify the recognitions that remain unchanged under it.  *Stabilize Context* witnessing *Nucleate* produces ***Fix Nucleus*** ([FixNucleus](fix-nucleus.md)).

This composition stays within earned terms and records contexts that the nucleus fixes, so *Fix Nucleus* follows.
