# Enrichment Hom

Hom-objects in an enriched category live in the base enrichment.  For the fixed
setting, we let the morphisms observe the invariant lattice:
`(FixedMorphism FixedInvariantLattice)` earns
***EnrichmentHom***.

The act yields lattice-valued hom-objects capturing all fixed morphisms from one
recognition to another.
