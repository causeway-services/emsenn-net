# Reentering Observation

## Interpretation

The valued observation must re-enter the observation witness.  *Recognize Observation* therefore witnesses *Valued Step Observation*, producing ***Reenter Observation*** ([ReenterObservation](reenter-observation.md)).

This records the return of observed values into the observation recognition, justifying *Reenter Observation*.
