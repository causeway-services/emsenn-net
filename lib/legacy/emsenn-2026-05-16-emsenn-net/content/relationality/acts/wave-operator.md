# Wave Operator

Wave propagation follows the second-order dynamics of the Laplacian acting on
harmonic modes.  We obtain the wave operator by composing the heat kernel with
the harmonic mode:
`(HeatKernel HarmonicMode)` earns
***WaveOperator***.
