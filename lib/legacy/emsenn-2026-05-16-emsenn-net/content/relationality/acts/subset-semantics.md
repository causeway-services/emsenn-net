# Structuring Semantic Subset

## Interpretation

A recognition-complete subset inherits the entire semantic structure.  *Structure Semantics* witnessing *Include Recognition* yields ***Subset Semantics*** ([SubsetSemantics](subset-semantics.md)).

This act records that inclusion-preserving subsets with inherited operations form substructures, so *Subset Semantics* is valid.
