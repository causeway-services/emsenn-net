# Closing Temporally

## Interpretation

From an idempotent flow we obtain temporal modalities where the “always” modality is Fix(F) meet x and the “eventually” modality is the join of the iterates Fⁿ(x).  *Fix Flow* witnessing *Nucleate* yields ***Close Temporally*** ([CloseTemporally](close-temporally.md)).

This act combines flow invariants with nuclei, recording the temporal modalities, so *Close Temporally* is valid.
