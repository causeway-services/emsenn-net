# Flowing ObservationEquivalence

## Interpretation

Closed observation-equivalence traces must flow through evaluation contexts.  *Build Evaluation Context* therefore witnesses *Trace Observation Equivalence Closure*, yielding ***Flow Observation Equivalence*** ([FlowObservationEquivalence](flow-observation-equivalence.md)).

The composition records those flows, forcing *Flow Observation Equivalence*.
