# Tailing JudgementEquivalence

## Interpretation

The equivalence trace has a remainder that must be captured.  *Tail Trace* witnessing *Trace Judgement Equivalence* yields ***Tail Judgement Equivalence*** ([TailJudgementEquivalence](tail-judgement-equivalence.md)).

This records the remaining equivalence acts, so *Tail Judgement Equivalence* follows.
