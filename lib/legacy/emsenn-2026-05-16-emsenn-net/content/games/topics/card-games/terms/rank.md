---
title: "rank"
aliases:
  - "rank"
date-created: 2026-03-01T00:00:00
---

The comparative value position of a card within a suit or game-specific order.
Rank order is not universal; games may elevate aces, invert low cards, or use
special priority hierarchies. Always read rank as a local rule definition tied
to one ruleset, not a fixed property across all card games.
