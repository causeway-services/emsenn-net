Key concepts in semiotics.

**Peircean terms:**

- [[sign]] — a triadic relation among representamen, object, and interpretant
- [[representamen]] — the form a sign takes (the sign vehicle)
- [[interpretant]] — the meaning produced by a sign
- [[semiosis]] — the process of sign-mediated meaning-making
- [[icon]] — a sign that resembles its object
- [[index-sign|index]] — a sign connected to its object by causal or spatial relation
- [[symbol]] — a sign connected to its object by convention

**Saussurean terms:**

- [[signifier]] — the form of a sign (Saussure's model)
- [[signified]] — the concept evoked by a signifier (Saussure's model)
