my ontology

My ontology, that is, the ontology of emsenn, is a hybrid of Lakota and Taoist ontologies, formalized with liberal epistemology.

  • my ontology isn't a taxonomy but a map of derivatives
    • not "what is being" but "what must necessarily derive from being"
  • dynamic and procedural
    • existing induces changing, necessitating more existing
  • formal
  • relational
    • the fundamental unit is not substance but a relation
  • differential
    • see derivatives
  • recursive
  • reflexive
  • aporiatic
    • a la deconstructionism, there is no resolving the fundamental claims
  • paraconsistent or inconsistent
    • influenced by dialetheism
  • semantic deflationism
  • negative ontology
  • abyssal ontology
  • process ontology
  • haecceity
